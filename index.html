01 / NOT A HOTEL KIND OF MOOD

Come as
you are.

Some come solo. Some come with a friend. Most arrive with a backpack and a loose plan.

Vyom is a relaxed base for days spent wandering Pushkar and evenings spent swapping stories. Grab a bed, meet the people passing through and make the place yours for a bit.

The best plans
are made
over chai.

That first hello in the common room. The pool rematch. A recommendation scribbled on a napkin. That's the hostel thing.
